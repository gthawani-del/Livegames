How to play & voice commands

Select a chip and tap Dragon, Tiger, or Tie. Tap Deal cards to lock bets. The higher card wins; matching ranks are a Tie. Sound uses local prerecorded game cues. Where speech input is supported, tap Speak and say “select 25”, “bet Dragon”, “deal”, “undo”, “clear bets”, “reset demo”, “help”, or “repeat”.
