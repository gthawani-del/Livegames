# Visual target

The room is a contemporary premium Asian casino broadcast studio: deep emerald felt, black lacquered architectural panels, restrained aged-brass trim and warm amber practical lighting. The finish should feel expensive and cinematic without becoming a fantasy temple.

The dealer is the hero. The rear wall uses two low-contrast sculptural reliefs—Dragon at camera-left and Tiger at camera-right—to identify the game sides. They must not create silhouette noise around the dealer's face, shoulders, hands, shoe or cards. Spartan Games branding is limited to the entrance sign, table rail medallion and result display.

The production view must show the dealer closer than the old video version. At the locked portrait camera, the dealer's head, torso, both hands, camera-left shoe, Dragon landing card and Tiger landing card must all remain visible. Card rank and suit must be readable through a synchronized in-stage magnifier, while the physical 3D card remains correctly scaled.

The cinematic arrival travels through a real 3 m-wide portal and the same studio geometry. It ends at the gameplay camera without a cut to an unrelated background. Returning players may skip the entry after the room has loaded.

The strangest element is the paired Dragon/Tiger relief system. Its promise is to clarify the two betting sides; if it competes with the dealer or appears like a fake doorway, it fails Function.
